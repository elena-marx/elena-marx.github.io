I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the <a href="https://lcl.ceu.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Language Comprehension Lab (LCL)</a> at the Central European University (CEU).

I investigate how language shapes the way people represent events and time. My research combines methods from psycholinguistics and cognitive science to examine how linguistic structure influences event representation during language comprehension. I study these questions across languages and modalities.

Currently, I am part of an <a href="https://www.ceu.edu/unit/center-belief-updating-and-debiasing-ceu-de-bias-center/research-groups" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">FWF-funded project</a> investigating the role of language in belief formation and belief updating.
