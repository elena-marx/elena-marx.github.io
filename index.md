I am a Postdoctoral Researcher working at the [Language Comprehension Lab (LCL)](https://lcl.ceu.edu/) at the Central European University (CEU).

I investigate how language shapes our understanding of events and time. My research combines psycholinguistics and cognitive science to examine the relationship between linguistic structure and event representation in language comprehension. I do all this by looking at many different languages and across modalities.

Currently I work on an [FWF project](https://www.ceu.edu/unit/center-belief-updating-and-debiasing-ceu-de-bias-center/research-groups) where I look at the role of language in belief formation.
